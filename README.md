# Retail Integrity Console

**Find retail issues. Assign ownership. Resolve them. Understand the impact.**

Retail Integrity Console is a retail operations solution designed to help teams manage problems that fall between systems and departments—such as incorrect prices, stock discrepancies, and outdated product information.

When those issues are reported through emails, spreadsheets, or messages, they can get lost. Staff may not know who is responsible, managers may not know what remains unresolved, and the same problem can happen again.

**The goal is simple:** give retail teams one place to record issues, assign responsibility, track fixes, and see which problems are costing the business time or money.

## The Problem

Imagine a product listed at the wrong price online while the store has the correct price. A team member notices it and sends a message. Someone else is supposed to make the change, but the request gets buried. Days later, the same error is still affecting orders.

This illustrates a broader operational problem:

- **Issues are scattered:** Staff report problems across different tools and conversations.
- **Ownership is unclear:** Nobody has a reliable view of who is fixing what.
- **Resolution is hard to verify:** A reported fix is not always checked or documented.
- **Business impact is difficult to see:** Managers may not know which issues are recurring or creating avoidable costs.

## The Solution

Retail Integrity Console is being designed around a straightforward workflow:

**Report an issue → Assign an owner → Prioritize the fix → Verify resolution → Monitor results**

The proposed application will bring together:

- **Issue tracking:** Record pricing, inventory, product-data, and operational problems in one place.
- **Clear accountability:** Assign owners, set priorities, and track progress through resolution.
- **Management dashboard:** See outstanding issues, how long they have been open, and which problems recur.
- **Financial visibility:** Estimate the potential cost or exposure of an issue using clearly defined assumptions.
- **AI-assisted workflows:** Explore issue categorization, concise summaries, and suggested next steps with human approval.

## Results That Matter

The value of the solution will be assessed through practical operating measures:

| Business question | Proposed measure |
| --- | --- |
| Are fewer issues being left unresolved? | Open issue backlog |
| Are teams resolving issues faster? | Time to verified resolution |
| Are the same problems happening repeatedly? | Recurrence rate |
| Does each issue have a clear owner? | Ownership and assignment coverage |
| Are fixes being checked? | Resolution verification rate |
| Which issues need management attention? | Estimated exposure and issue priority |

These are **success measures**, not claims of results already achieved.
