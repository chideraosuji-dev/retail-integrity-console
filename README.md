# Retail Integrity Console

**Retail Operations Intelligence | Exception Management | Financial Oversight**

Retail Integrity Console is a retail operations solution focused on identifying operational exceptions, improving accountability, standardizing resolution workflows, and strengthening management visibility into operational and financial risk.

The solution brings together business systems analysis, workflow design, and software engineering to address recurring issues in inventory, pricing, product data, and cross-functional execution.

## Business Challenge

Retail operations often depend on multiple platforms, teams, and communication channels. When exception handling is fragmented, discrepancies can remain unresolved, accountability becomes unclear, and management lacks a consistent view of operational exposure.

Retail Integrity Console addresses this challenge through a structured approach to detecting, assigning, resolving, and monitoring operational exceptions.

## Solution Scope

The proposed solution is organized around four capabilities:

- **Exception management:** Centralized issue records, ownership, prioritization, status tracking, escalation, and resolution validation.
- **Operational intelligence:** Visibility into open exceptions, issue aging, recurring problems, and resolution performance.
- **Financial oversight:** Estimated exposure and correction costs, supported by transparent calculation assumptions.
- **Intelligent assistance:** Potential classification, case summaries, and recommended follow-up actions, subject to human review and validation.

These capabilities define the intended product scope and will be validated incrementally during implementation.
