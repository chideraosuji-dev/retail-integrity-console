# Stakeholders and Responsibilities

## Why Stakeholders Matter

An operational issue often passes through several people before it is resolved. Retail Integrity Console needs to make each person's role clear: **who reports the problem, who fixes it, who checks the result, and who needs visibility**.

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | What they need | Role in the proposed workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Store Operations Manager** | Know which store issues need attention and whether they are resolved | Prioritize issues, assign operational ownership, review results |
| **Inventory and Fulfillment Coordinator** | Resolve stock discrepancies and avoid repeated inventory problems | Investigate stock-related cases and confirm corrections |
| **E-commerce / Systems Support Lead** | Receive complete, actionable requests and control system changes | Assess technical issues, implement authorized changes, document outcomes |
| **Retail Owner / General Manager** | See business impact, recurring problems, and overall resolution performance | Set priorities, review dashboards, approve material decisions |
| **Store Associates** | Report problems quickly without chasing multiple people | Raise issues and provide useful details or supporting evidence |
| **Customers** | Receive reliable price, product, stock, and order information | Benefit from better accuracy and fewer service disruptions |

## A Practical Ownership Example

A product is displayed at the wrong price online:

1. A **store associate** records the discrepancy and attaches the correct price.
2. The **store manager** confirms priority and assigns responsibility.
3. **Systems support** updates the relevant product record.
4. An authorized reviewer **verifies** that the correct price appears.
5. The **retail manager** can see that the issue was resolved and whether similar errors keep occurring.

The specific assignments and approvals will be finalized through requirements validation.

## Priority Questions for Discovery

- Who is allowed to report, assign, update, and close an issue?
- Which issues require management approval?
- What evidence is needed before marking an issue resolved?
- When must an unresolved issue be escalated?
- What information should managers see in daily or weekly reporting?

## Expected Outcome

A defined responsibility model that reduces unclear ownership, missed handoffs, and corrections that cannot be verified.

*Scenario note: Stakeholder roles are defined using the illustrative Northlane Retail Group scenario and will require validation in a live deployment.*
