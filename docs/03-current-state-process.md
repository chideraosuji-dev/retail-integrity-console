# Current-State Process Analysis

## The Workflow Problem

Retail issues can be reported in one place, acted on in another, and resolved without a clear record of what happened. The challenge is not necessarily a lack of retail software; it is the missing connection between **identifying a problem and confirming that it was fixed**.

## Example: Incorrect Product Price

A store team notices a price discrepancy between the store and the online catalog. The issue is sent by message to a supervisor and forwarded to systems support. The change may be completed, but the person who raised it has no reliable way to confirm the result.

This creates opportunities for delays, duplicate follow-ups, unclear ownership, and repeat errors.

## Current-State Workflow

The assumed process is:

1. **Identify:** A staff member notices a pricing, inventory, product-data, or operational issue.
2. **Report:** The issue is sent through email, chat, a spreadsheet, or an informal conversation.
3. **Forward:** A supervisor or colleague passes the request to another person or team.
4. **Interpret:** The recipient determines what needs fixing, how urgent it is, and whether enough information was provided.
5. **Correct:** An authorized person applies the change in the relevant system or process.
6. **Follow up:** The original requestor may need to ask whether the work was done.
7. **Recheck:** Verification is inconsistent, making recurring issues harder to identify.

## Where the Process Breaks Down

| Weakness | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Requests are spread across multiple channels | Issues can be overlooked or recorded more than once |
| No clear owner | Staff may assume someone else is handling the problem |
| Inconsistent priority | High-impact issues may wait behind less urgent work |
| Limited status visibility | Managers cannot reliably see what remains open |
| Weak resolution checks | A task can be considered closed without confirming the correction |
| Little recurrence tracking | Teams may repeatedly fix the symptom instead of addressing its cause |

## Improvement Direction

The proposed future-state workflow is:

**Log the issue → Assign responsibility → Prioritize → Apply correction → Verify → Close → Review patterns**

A single record would connect the issue, its owner, supporting evidence, actions taken, verification, and status.

## Measures to Track

- Open issues and issue age
- Time to verified resolution
- Repeat issue frequency
- Percentage of cases with an assigned owner
- Percentage of cases resolved with verification
- Manual follow-up required per issue

## Summary

The central opportunity is to make problem resolution **visible, accountable, and measurable**, from the first report to the final check.

*Scenario note: This is a modeled current-state workflow for Northlane Retail Group, used as a design starting point rather than a verified observation of a real retailer.*
